Photograph the handwritten score

One photo per page, in page order. Lay each page flat, fill the frame, and avoid shadows. Works with full scores (multiple staves) — one staff becomes one part.

Tip: on a phone, tap the button once per page; on a computer you can pick several photos at once.

      What's an API key?

      Think of it as a password that lets this app use Google's music-reading AI on your behalf. Without it, the app has no way to send your photo off to be transcribed.

      It's free. If you have a Google account (Gmail counts), Google already includes a generous free allowance for this kind of thing — no credit card, no trial, no catch. Reading a page of sheet music uses a tiny fraction of it.

      Your key stays yours. It's stored only in this browser on your device, and it's sent to Google — and only to Google — when you tap Transcribe.

      1. Go to Google AI Studio and sign in with your Google account.
      2. Click Get API key → Create API key, then copy it.
      3. Paste it into the API key box — about two minutes total, and you're done.
